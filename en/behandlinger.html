MicroneedlingCollagen Induction Therapy

60 minFrom DKK 680
Book now

Microneedling is a skin-rejuvenating treatment in which fine needles create tiny micro-channels in the skin and stimulate its natural production of collagen and elastin. The treatment is adapted to each skin type, skin condition and treatment area, with the needle depth adjusted to the skin's needs and the desired result.

The treatment can help improve the skin's texture, firmness and elasticity and reduce the visibility of fine lines, acne scars and uneven skin tone. The result is smoother skin with a more even texture and renewed radiance.

Diamond MicrodermabrasionDiamantslibning

50 minFrom DKK 485
Book now

Microdermabrasion is a gentle diamond exfoliation that removes dead skin cells, improves the skin's surface texture and gives a fresher, more radiant glow. The treatment helps smooth the skin's surface and leaves it smoother, softer and more even.

Includes
  • Cleansing
  • Diamond Microdermabrasion
  • Moisturising mask
  • Finishing day cream with SPF
Especially recommended if you want to improve
  • Sun-damaged skin
  • Superficial pigmentation changes
  • Dull, tired-looking skin
  • Uneven skin texture
  • Clogged pores
  • Superficial acne scars and scarring
  • Large pores and rough skin surface
  • Fine lines

Please note: The treatment is less suitable for very thin, fragile or sensitive skin with reduced elasticity, as the combination of exfoliation and vacuum suction can increase the risk of irritation and bruising.
