About me

About me and my background

Education
Authorised skin therapist
Diploma
CIDESCO international diploma

I am an authorised cosmetologist (Kosmetolog) and skin therapist (Hudterapeut), educated in Stockholm, Sweden, where I began my studies in early 2022 at one of Stockholm's most well-established schools specialising in professional skincare education. I completed my studies in 2024, qualifying as an authorised skin therapist.

As part of my education, I also obtained an international CIDESCO diploma, an internationally recognised qualification in professional skincare and beauty therapy.

After graduating, I worked as a skin therapist in Sweden before eventually deciding to move to Denmark to pursue my vision of opening my own skincare studio.

Today, I offer a range of treatments, from classic and results-driven facials to more advanced treatments such as microneedling. I always strive to tailor each treatment to the individual needs and condition of the skin. My goal is to continue developing professionally and gradually expand my treatment menu with new treatments and techniques.

I have a genuine passion for skin health, professional skincare and helping people feel comfortable and confident in their own skin. For me, skincare is about more than just aesthetics. It is about understanding the skin, recognising its needs and knowing how to care for it properly.

My vision

Healthy, strong and radiant skin with a natural glow

My vision is to help every client achieve skin that feels healthy, looks radiant and maintains its natural balance.

I believe that healthy skin starts with understanding the skin as part of the body as a whole. The skin is closely connected to our overall health and well-being, which is why I value a holistic approach to skincare. Rather than focusing solely on the surface, I believe in looking at the bigger picture and considering the different factors that can influence the skin.

I believe in thoughtful skincare, professional treatments and individually tailored solutions. Every skin is unique, which is why I believe it is important to understand your skin and what it truly needs.

By combining my education, professional experience and passion for skincare, I aim to provide treatments that deliver visible results while supporting the skin's long-term health.

For me, it is just as important that you feel comfortable, relaxed and well taken care of during your visit as it is that you leave the studio with skin that looks and feels its best.
